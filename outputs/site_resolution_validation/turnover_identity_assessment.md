# Turnover and identifier churn

The deterministic crosswalk contains 45 confirmed changed-identifier continuities (0 involving a 99 reporting-unit transition and 45 other exact-name/address identifier changes) and 125 probable continuities (4 involving 99); 125 rows require manual review. At the physical-site ≥20 threshold, summed consecutive-year entries/exits change from 159/116 under raw identifiers to 155/112 after confirmed continuity. These are 118/75 when probable links are additionally included. These are event counts across year pairs, not unique hospitals. Same identifiers are also recorded as confirmed continuity. Probable links are not silently promoted. Ownership changes, mergers, splits, and renamings cannot be reliably separated from the available aggregate evidence. Current turnover/Table S6 should therefore not be treated as validated.
