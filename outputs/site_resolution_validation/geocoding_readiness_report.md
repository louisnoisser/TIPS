# Geocoding readiness

The resolved history contains 670 identifiable physical-site identifiers and 605/2387 provider-year records with an explicit `Standortkontaktdaten` address; 859 inherit an explicit institution address and the remaining legacy records have unresolved contact hierarchy. Existing coordinates are available for 529/2387 provider-year records representing 113 site identifiers, but only 43 identifiers have coordinates demonstrably generated from a site-specific address; 25 provider-year records share a coordinate with another component report, and 0 lie outside the Germany bounding-box check. The cache is keyed to the 2024 cohort and does not establish historical site-specific coordinates. No manual correction registry was found. The current data are therefore not suitable for a validated longitudinal physical-site accessibility analysis. Site/institution addresses come from the eligible G-BA XML report contact blocks; coordinates come from `data/cache/geocode/tips_8-839-8_geocode_cache.csv`; boundary paths are configured in `src/gba_ops_reanalysis/config.py`. Isochrones were not recalculated.
